Next Review         : 2026-11-15

Compliance Requirements
    - Suppliers must comply with Singapore food safety and
      environmental regulations.
    - Halal certification required for relevant product lines.
    - Suppliers must declare use of palm oil and prefer RSPO
      certified sources where available.
    - Anti-bribery clauses included in all purchase contracts.

Supplier Audit Frequency
    - Strategic ingredient suppliers: every 18 months.
    - Packaging suppliers              : self-questionnaire only.

Escalation Procedure
    - Non-compliance escalated to Head of Procurement first.
    - Repeated breaches reviewed by Managing Director.
    - No formal Board-level oversight defined yet.

Signed:  D. Koh, Head of Procurement   2024-11-15
