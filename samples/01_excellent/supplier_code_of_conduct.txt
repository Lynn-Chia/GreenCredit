1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This policy applies to all suppliers, sub-contractors and service
providers engaged by Acme Green Manufacturing globally and forms
part of every purchase order and master services agreement.

2. COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
Suppliers shall:
    a. Maintain ISO 14001 (or equivalent) alignment for any site
       producing more than 100 tCO2e/year on Acme's behalf.
    b. Prohibit child labour, forced labour and human trafficking
       (ILO Conventions 138 and 182).
    c. Submit an annual conflict-minerals declaration (3TG).
    d. Comply with the Acme Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption Policy.
    e. Disclose Scope 1 + Scope 2 emissions where revenue from
       Acme exceeds S$ 500,000 per year.

3. SUPPLIER AUDIT FREQUENCY
    Tier-1 strategic suppliers: audited annually (on-site).
    Tier-2 suppliers           : audited every 24 months.
    Tier-3 suppliers           : self-assessment questionnaire
                                  every 36 months.
Auditors are independent and accredited (e.g., SGS, BV, DNV).

4. ESCALATION PROCEDURE
    Step 1: Non-conformance logged and CAP issued (30 days).
    Step 2: Failure to close CAP -> escalation to CSO.
    Step 3: Two strikes within 12 months -> Board ESG Committee
            review; possible contract suspension or termination.
    Step 4: Material breaches reported to MAS-aligned green
            financing counterparties.

5. WHISTLEBLOWING
A 24/7 anonymous channel (run by EthicsPoint) is available to
suppliers and their employees. Retaliation is prohibited.
